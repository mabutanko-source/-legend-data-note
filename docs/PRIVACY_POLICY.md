# Privacy Policy

## Legend Data App - Privacy Policy

Your data is protected and we comply with all privacy regulations.

### Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

### Information We Collect

- **User Account Information**: Email, name, phone number
- **Transaction Data**: Purchase history, wallet transactions
- **Device Information**: Device type, OS version for optimization

### Data Usage

We use your information to:
- Process transactions
- Improve our services
- Send important updates
- Prevent fraud

### Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.
